# Avatar HOI4 Redux

A modernized and expanded continuation of the **Avatar: The Last Airbender** total-conversion mod for **Hearts of Iron IV**.

## Project goals

- Modernize the 2024 Workshop release for current Hearts of Iron IV versions.
- Preserve and build on the original map, countries, technologies, economy, characters, units, decisions, and scenarios.
- Fix legacy bugs, broken references, unfinished localisation, and obsolete systems.
- Improve naval and aerial warfare so they matter strategically even when the Fire Nation is the dominant conventional power.
- Expand narrative content, decisions, events, bookmarks, and alternate-history possibilities while keeping canon-inspired starts coherent.
- Keep the Avatar as a world mechanic rather than a normal playable country.

## Development branches

- `main` — stable project documentation and integrated work.
- `modernization` — engine compatibility, bug fixing, cleanup, and technical migration.
- `content` — new events, decisions, mechanics, bookmarks, balance, and narrative content.

## Initial modernization priorities

1. Boot reliably on the current HOI4 version.
2. Audit `descriptor.mod`, `replace_path` usage, units, technologies, characters, abilities, and interface files.
3. Fix broken country references and empty country definitions.
4. Validate bookmarks and the 99 AG scenario.
5. Clean broken/duplicate localisation and visible TODO text.
6. Test through HOI4 logs and fix P0/P1 runtime errors.

## Planned gameplay work

- Fire Nation imperial logistics and convoy dependence.
- Earth Kingdom sea-denial doctrine and coastal defense.
- Water Tribe littoral/polar naval warfare.
- Expanded piracy and convoy raiding.
- Air warfare progression from reconnaissance balloons to military airships.
- Day of Black Sun and Sozin's Comet world mechanics.
- Expanded Avatar, White Lotus, Dai Li, resistance, and royal-family systems.
- Additional bookmarks after the 99 AG scenario is stable.

## Upstream

This project is based on the 2024 Workshop release of **Avatar - The last Airbender**, created by **Spielkrokodil** and contributors. The original Workshop description explicitly invited successors and stated that the project was free for others to continue. This repository is an independent continuation/redux and is not presented as the original team's official repository.
